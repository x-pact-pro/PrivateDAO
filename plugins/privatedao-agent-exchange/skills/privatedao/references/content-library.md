# PrivateDAO Content Library

Use this file as a source-backed creative brief library. It is for product marketing, partner communication, investor-facing summaries, social posts, image prompts, and video prompts.

## Approved product facts

### PrivateDAO

PrivateDAO helps organizations run sensitive work through controlled workflows and share only the evidence needed to trust the result.

Official pages:

- Website: https://privatedao.org/
- Thesis: https://privatedao.org/thesis/
- Token: https://privatedao.org/token/
- GitHub: https://github.com/X-PACT/PrivateDAO

### Agent Exchange

PrivateDAO Agent Exchange is a commercial MCP-compatible marketplace with 27 current services: PrivatePAY x402 plus 26 verification, intelligence, research, transaction, receipt, and agent-discovery services.

- Marketplace: https://agents.privatedao.org/marketplace
- MCP: https://agents.privatedao.org/mcp
- OpenAPI: https://agents.privatedao.org/openapi.json
- Service catalog: https://agents.privatedao.org/services

### PrivatePAY x402

PrivatePAY x402 is a live Agent Exchange service for privacy-preserving x402 payment challenges and settlement status.

- Service page: https://agents.privatedao.org/services/privatepay-x402
- Network: Solana Mainnet
- Execution rail: MagicBlock private rollup settlement
- Public evidence described by the service: request commitment and settlement status
- Customer flow: understand the quote, approve payment, complete the service, verify the receipt

Use measured language: privacy-preserving, reduced linkability, controlled disclosure, and private settlement where supported. Do not write untraceable, invisible, guaranteed anonymous, risk-free, or guaranteed returns.

## Content templates

### Product launch post

Write a [language] [platform] post for [audience] introducing [product]. Include: the customer problem, the concrete outcome, one verified proof point, the official link, and one action. Keep it within [length].

### PrivatePAY post

### Investor or partner brief

Create a one-page brief covering: product, buyer, workflow, evidence, business model, integrations, current limits, and next commercial step. Use only verified facts from the official pages and label anything that still needs confirmation.

### Image brief

Create a visual brief for [product] in [aspect ratio]. Specify: focal subject, scene, composition, PrivateDAO brand placement, headline and supporting copy, palette, lighting, accessibility, export sizes, and a negative prompt. Use the supplied symbol or lockup; keep text short and legible. Do not create fake badges, fake dashboards, fake customer logos, or fabricated chain confirmations.

### Short video brief

Create a [15/30/60]-second video brief for [audience]. Include: hook, shot list with timestamps, voiceover, on-screen captions, transition direction, product URL, end card, and a version without voiceover. Show a customer journey from problem to verified outcome. Avoid displaying private keys, wallet seeds, fake transaction hashes, or unsupported privacy claims.

## Platform adaptation

- X: one strong opening line, two or three short proof lines, one link, optional restrained tags.
- LinkedIn: operational problem, business impact, evidence, and a low-friction demo or contact CTA.
- Telegram: announcement, who it helps, what to click, and a direct service link.
- Website: headline, customer outcome, proof block, workflow, pricing or quote path, and FAQ.
- Agent catalog: concise service description, input, output, approval boundary, and MCP/OpenAPI link.

## Quality check

Before returning content, verify: product name, URL, network, privacy language, audience, platform format, call to action, and whether every factual claim has an official source. Mark drafts as promotional copy when they are not evidence reports.

# Skill: PrivateDAO Overview

## Purpose

Answer customer and investor questions about PrivateDAO without leading with protocol jargon.

## Simple explanation

PrivateDAO turns sensitive organizational work into a controlled workflow: define the rule, keep private inputs protected, approve the action, execute through the suitable rail, reconcile the result, and share a receipt or proof instead of exposing the whole process.

## Product families

- Proof Workflows: verification, evidence, receipts, and audit-ready outcomes.
- Private Governance: proposals, committees, participation, and accountable decisions.
- Treasury Coordination: budgets, approvals, execution, settlement, and reconciliation.
- Agent Exchange: machine-readable services for discovery, execution, payment-aware jobs, and receipt verification.
- PrivatePAY x402: quote-first private payment workflows for compatible digital services.

## Response rule

Lead with the outcome for an organization. Mention chains and providers only when the question requires implementation or evidence detail.

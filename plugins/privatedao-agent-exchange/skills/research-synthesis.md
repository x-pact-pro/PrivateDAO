# Skill: Research and Evidence Synthesis

Combine sources without overstating certainty:

- `agent.research.report` for a structured research report.
- `decision.context` for a decision-oriented summary.
- `intelligence.synthesize` for evidence synthesis.
- `market.snapshot` for current market context.
- `launch.check` for launch-readiness signals.

Separate source facts, derived interpretation, missing evidence, and recommended next actions. Do not turn analysis into investment advice.

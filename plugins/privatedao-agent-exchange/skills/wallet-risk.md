# Skill: Wallet and Risk Intelligence

For wallet or token questions, separate facts from signals:

- `wallet.intelligence`: wallet activity and context.
- `research.wallet`: behavior analysis.
- `wallet.bot.classify`: bot-pattern classification.
- `portfolio.intelligence`: portfolio context.
- `token.intelligence`: token facts and intelligence.
- `risk.score`: structured risk signal.
- `anomaly.detect`: unusual activity signal.

Never describe a risk score as a guarantee, financial advice, or proof of future performance.

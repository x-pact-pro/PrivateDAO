# PrivatePAY x402 Profile

## Live Agent Exchange service

PrivatePAY x402 is listed as a live Agent Exchange service at:

`https://agents.privatedao.org/services/privatepay-x402`

The current service surface identifies the route as Mainnet Live, x402, and Solana Mainnet. It documents MagicBlock private rollup settlement, a request commitment, and settlement status. Use the live service page as the product reference when explaining the payment flow.

Public product page: https://privatedao.org/privatepay

PrivatePAY x402 is a quote-first payment and service-access flow for compatible digital services. It is designed to make payment approval, private execution where supported, service delivery, and receipt verification understandable to a normal customer and usable by an AI agent.

The public-facing promise is controlled privacy and verifiable completion, not untraceability. Public summaries should avoid exposing payment identifiers, payer details, exact amounts, settlement timing, or provider internals unless required by the user.

The Agent Exchange MCP surface is:

`https://agents.privatedao.org/mcp`

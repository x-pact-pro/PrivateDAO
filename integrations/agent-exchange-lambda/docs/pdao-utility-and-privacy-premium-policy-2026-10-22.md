# PDAO Utility and Privacy Premium Policy

## Live utility

PDAO powers access, credits, discounts, bounded agent benefits, seller benefits,
holder reputation, governance coordination, and commercial activation. The
exact mint and live balance are verified at execution time where a benefit is
eligible. PDAO is never transferred or locked for ordinary holder access.

The ordinary Agent Exchange payment asset remains USDC on Solana Mainnet.
PDAO does not replace USDC settlement, does not authorize treasury operations,
and does not grant signing or withdrawal authority.

## Fixed holder promotion

The promotion is a server-defined platform window, not a wallet-specific
30-day period. It requires at least $25 of the exact PDAO mint at each eligible
service execution, requires no payment, and has no seven-day requirement.
Holder Rewards are separate and require seven consecutive qualifying days before
monthly credits and discounts are issued.

## Privacy premium proposal

The Privacy Premium Governance Proposal dated 2026-10-22 is represented in the
machine-readable `pdao_utility.privacy_premium` field. It is intentionally
`governance_pending` and is not charged in production until governance approves
parameters and an on-chain PDAO payment verifier is independently tested.

The proposed scope is limited to PrivatePAY x402 and future confidential
workflows. Public x402 and ordinary Agent Exchange jobs remain USDC or existing
holder-credit paths. MagicBlock fees, network fees, RPC costs, and operational
infrastructure remain separate SOL/stablecoin or operating costs.

This separation prevents a missing PDAO oracle or an unverified token transfer
from interrupting ordinary payments or creating a false privacy-premium claim.
